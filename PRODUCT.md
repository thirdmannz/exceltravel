# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated: static HTML/CSS/JavaScript for the first working prototype, chosen to minimize setup and make iteration fast. This can be migrated into an existing framework later if required.

## Users
Primary audience is inferred from the existing Excel Travel / 赛尔旅游 site content: Chinese-speaking visitors planning travel in New Zealand, spanning first-time visitors, families, older travellers, small groups, and travellers who want local Chinese-language support.

## Product Purpose
Help Chinese-speaking travellers understand and book New Zealand travel services with confidence. The homepage should quickly communicate what Excel Travel offers, surface real trip products and service categories, and move visitors toward enquiry or booking.

## Positioning
A New Zealand-focused Chinese-language travel service combining packaged tours, private/custom itineraries, flight-related help, and local support. The differentiator is practical Chinese-language assistance around real New Zealand itineraries rather than a generic global travel marketplace.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse on desktop and mobile, compare trip options, review itinerary/pricing/policy details, then enquire or book online. Existing business content includes Christchurch departures, group tours, custom itineraries, online booking, deposits, and booking/cancellation policies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Preserve the Excel Travel / 赛尔旅游 brand name unless the user explicitly changes it.
- Prototype must work as a responsive web page without requiring a build system.
- Use only real business facts already present in the source material or clearly generic interface copy.
- Do not invent testimonials, awards, customer counts, licensing claims, exact prices, or policy terms not evidenced in existing content.
- Existing service categories to foreground: small/group tours, custom travel, selected tours, and flight/travel assistance.
- Existing booking signals include online booking and deposit-based products.

## Brand Commitments
- Existing names: Excel Travel / 赛尔旅游.
- Chinese-first communication with clear, approachable travel language.
- User preference carried into this redesign: cards should have more character, with shadows appearing primarily on hover rather than permanently.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing scraped/live-site content previously inspected in this project includes product pricing structures in NZD, online-booking flags, Christchurch meeting/departure location text, deposit details, and a detailed Chinese booking/cancellation policy. No verified testimonials or quantified social-proof metrics are currently available, so the prototype must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Make trip discovery effortless: show a small number of understandable paths instead of overwhelming visitors.
2. Earn trust with concrete travel information and transparent actions rather than invented marketing claims.
3. Keep Chinese-language service and local New Zealand relevance immediately visible.
4. Prefer strong responsive hierarchy and usable interactions over decorative complexity.
5. Use motion and card effects to clarify interactivity, not to distract from booking decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
